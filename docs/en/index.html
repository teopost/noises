Noises is the practice studio for musicians that runs on your own server: isolate vocals, drums and bass with AI, change pitch and tempo in real time, read chords and sheet music, get a click that really follows the band, loop the passages you are working on and record your part over the song.

Features

Everything you need to learn a song

From the first listen to the backing track for the gig, all in one place. No subscriptions, and no uploading your music to someone else's cloud.

AI stem separation

Demucs v4 splits the song into vocals, drums, bass and other, or into 6 tracks with guitar and piano. For the cleanest vocals there's Mel-Band RoFormer. Mute, solo and volume per track.

Live pitch and tempo

±12 semitones and 50–150% speed, independent and applied in real time in the browser. The song's key updates as you transpose.

Chords

170 chords recognized with BTC, even on the unsplit song. Current and next chord, C D E or Do Re Mi notation, manual corrections.

Notes and piano roll

Automatic transcription of every track, an editable piano roll and a synth with eight sounds to hear the notes, even an octave up.

Sheet music and tabs

MusicXML for piano, drums, guitar and 4- or 5-string bass, with a cursor that follows the music. Print, PDF and export.

Adaptive click

A metronome that follows the drummer's real hits, or the average tempo, or a fixed BPM. A count-in that always lands on the "1".

Multitrack editor

Projects with several songs, clips to cut and move, groups, bookmarks, undo and redo. Drag files from your computer or import from YouTube.

Setlists for the stage

Line up the projects for the night: when a song ends the next one starts after the pause you choose, or waits for your Play.

Ready-made backing tracks

Export only the active tracks to MP3, WAV, FLAC or MIDI, for example everything except your instrument, with click and count-in included.

Rhythm

A click that stays in time with the band

Real drummers don't play like a metronome. Noises detects every kick drum hit and locks the click to the moment the hit is actually heard.

  • Time signature and downbeat adjustable: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8, 12/8, double or half time.
  • Fine offset by ear, 5 ms at a time, saved for each song.
  • Six sounds: beep, woodblock, claves, cowbell, hi-hat, rimshot.
  • Count-in of one or two bars that always lands on the downbeat, even when starting mid-bar.
  • In exported backing tracks the click is exactly the one you hear.
Practice

Loop your solo until you nail it

Select the passage you want to work on, give it a name and recall it with one click. The loop is already on and the playhead at the start: press Play and play along, even slowed down.

  • Saved sections in the project: "Solo 1", "Bridge", "Final riff". The button shows the current section, the little ▾ arrow opens the list.
  • Loop on the fly: while it plays press I at the start of the passage and O at the end, and the loop starts right away.
  • Fine adjustment of start and end: drag the edges (hold Ctrl to snap to the click), 0.1 s or 0.01 s per click, or snap to the previous or next click. Even while playing, without stopping.
  • Bar lines in the timeline, in time with the click.
  • They stay in place at any speed: learn the solo at 70% and then bring it up to tempo.
  • Count-in on every restart, so you come in right on the "1".

Recording

Record your part over the band

Add an empty track, pick the input and press ⏺. You play over the song and the recording lands in the timeline, already lined up with everything else.

  • Microphone or audio interface: input 1, 2 or stereo, with a VU meter to set the level before you start.
  • Live waveform while you record, with audio latency compensated automatically.
  • Multiple takes on the same track: a new take replaces only the part you redid, the rest stays. Undo to go back to the previous one.
  • Listen to yourself instead of the original, compare, and export the result like any other track.

Saves and sharing

Prepare your mixes and let everyone hear them

Save the project state under a name, for example Vocals only or Guitar solo backing, and recall it with one click. Every save can become a public link to send to the band or post on social media.

  • Saves of volumes, mute/solo, key, speed and loop. The save in use picks up your changes on its own: move the loop and it stays saved there.
  • Public link with an elegant page, no account needed: whoever opens it hears the mix with its loop, key and speed.
  • Several saves on one page: listeners switch between them without losing their place in the song.
  • Always up to date: change a save and its link updates by itself. Turn it off whenever you like.
  • Social previews with title, save, loop and the waveform of the mix.

From instrument to paper

The sheet music writes itself

Every separated track becomes sheet music: staff, tablature or both. It's a starting point to fix up in the piano roll, not a job to redo from scratch.

  • 4- or 5-string bass and guitar (Drop D and Drop C too), with fingerings that keep hand movement to a minimum.
  • Drums in percussion notation: kick, snare and hi-hat.
  • A cursor that follows playback, accidentals that match the key, MusicXML export and printing.

How it works

From file to stage in three steps

Load

Drag an audio file into the timeline or paste a YouTube link. The song goes into your library and into the project.

Split and analyze

From the track's ⋯ menu choose separation, chords, rhythm and notes. Everything runs on your server, even without internet.

Play

Slow down, transpose, save the hard passage as a section and loop it, take out your instrument, turn on the click and record yourself. Then export the backing track.

Install

Yours, on your hardware

A single Docker image with models and libraries already inside. It runs on a PC, a server or a 64-bit Raspberry Pi 4/5, for multiple users, each with their own library.

  • Accounts with email verification or Google sign-in.
  • Administration: users and roles, email invitations, sign-in and activity log, a notice when a new version is out.
  • Your data stays home: songs, projects and analyses live in a Docker volume.
  • Project backup to a zip file with all its songs: restore it on another installation and the songs go into its library.
  • Away from home over HTTPS, for example through Cloudflare Tunnel.
terminal
$ git clone https://github.com/teopost/noises.git
$ cd noises
$ make up      # build + start
$ make open    # http://localhost:8080
raspberrypi
$ git clone https://github.com/teopost/noises.git
$ cd noises && cp .env.example .env
# in .env: the prebuilt image from CI
NOISES_IMAGE=ghcr.io/teopost/noises:latest
$ make pull    # download and start, no build

Requirements: Docker with Compose v2 and make. The browser's audio engine needs HTTPS or localhost.
