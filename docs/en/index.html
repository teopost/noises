Everything to learn a song

Split the instruments

Vocals, drums, bass, guitar and piano, each on its own track.

Slow down and transpose

Change the speed without changing the key, or change the key to suit your voice.

Loop the hard part

Repeat that tricky solo until you nail it.

Chords and sheet music

Chords, notes and tabs written for you.

A click that follows the band

The metronome that keeps time with the real drummer.

Record and share

Record yourself over the song and send the mix to the band with a link.

Three steps

Add a song

Drop in a file or paste a YouTube link.

Split

Choose which instruments to isolate.

Play

Mute your instrument, slow down and play along.

Yours, at home

Install it on your computer, a server or a Raspberry Pi. Your music never goes to someone else's cloud.
